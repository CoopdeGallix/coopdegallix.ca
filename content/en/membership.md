FAQ:
  title: Frequently asked questions
  questions:
  - q: Do I need to be a member to shop at the co-op?
    r: No. Everyone can shop at the co-op, member or not. Some benefits are reserved for members.
  - q: Is one membership per household enough?
    r: Membership belongs to a person, not a household. Each individual who wants to become a user member fills out their own form and pays their own share.
  - q: Can children become members?
    r: Under the bylaws, children cannot become members, but they can use their parents' member card.
  - q: Do I have to renew every year?
    r: No. Once you have paid your membership share, you remain a member for as long as you leave it in the co-op.
  - q: Do I have to live in Gallix?
    r: Not at all. Anyone who wants to support the co-op can join, whether you are passing through, live nearby or live far away.
  - q: Where do I send an Interac e-Transfer?
    r: "Send it to **`adhesion@coopdegallix.ca`**."
